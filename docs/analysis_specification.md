# Frozen execution specification — 22 November 2026

Status: retrospective, exploratory analysis of previously inspected R8/R10 data. This is a timestamped execution specification, not an untouched preregistration. Earlier hypotheses and legacy external results were already known. No model results under this specification existed at drafting.

## Estimands and scope
Four separate R8 binary items retain original coding. Only cultural non-rejection and democracy preference are transported to R10. No total consciousness score. Economic redistribution and tax/service preferences are not interchangeable. Associations concern sampled national adult publics under an equal-country mixture, not a population-weighted continent. Original household weights are normalized to equal total country mass, with total weight equal to the analysis sample size. Alternative raw-country size weighting and unweighted analyses diagnose dependence on pseudo-likelihood scaling. No design-unbiased multilevel claim without true PSU and stage-specific selection weights.

## Variables and models
Parsimonious primary core: age in decades, female indicator, urban indicator (urban code 1 versus other including peri-urban), education code 0–9, mean of five deprivation items, count of access to six assets (codes 1 or 2), household electricity, and currently employed indicator (codes 2 or 3). Complete core cases are primary for inference and every matched predictive comparison; outcome missingness is item-specific. Country means and individual deviations are household-weighted, with categorical core variables represented as indicators. Primary contexts are lagged log GDP and V-Dem electoral democracy (v2x_polyarchy, interview year minus one), standardized using development data. These are not a complete institutional/history specification. Occupation is nominal, enters a sensitivity as indicators, and is never averaged as class rank. Core categories and missingness are archived.

Primary explanatory models: null logistic random intercept; within–between random intercept with eight deviations and eight country means plus GDP/institutions; random education slope independent of intercept, and education-deviation × electoral-democracy interaction. Gaussian effects, lme4 adaptive quadrature for random-intercept models (9 nodes), Laplace for random-slope models. Weighted Bernoulli likelihood is explicitly a pseudo-likelihood. Model-based fixed-effect intervals are conditional on these assumptions; cluster-robust country-fixed-effect logistic regressions provide a separate within-country check. No significance-based pruning. Singular fits and convergence warnings are reported.

## Validation and fairness
Known-country interpolation uses a fixed 75/25 random split stratified by country and outcome. All models use the same complete-core outcome sample. Pooled core logistic; country-only training prevalence; centred core logistic; multilevel core logistic with random country intercept; context-only GDP/institution model; and pooled core plus context. Centre known-country rows by training-only country means. No hyperparameter search.
Strict LOCO holds out each entire country and fits transformations on training countries. Country-only falls back to overall training prevalence. Individual centring uses the training grand mean for an unseen country, not target covariate means. The resulting centred model is labelled explicitly as the inductive fallback, not a test of ideal within-country centring. Unknown random intercepts are integrated over the training estimated Gaussian distribution, rather than estimated from target labels. A separate transductive sensitivity may use target covariate means, but is never called strict LOCO. Validation multilevel fits use Laplace (nAGQ=1); explanatory random-intercept quadrature and Laplace estimates are compared as numerical sensitivity.
Temporal evaluation fits on all R8 eligible observations, uses R8 country means and effects for shared countries, grand-mean/Gaussian integration for new countries; updates lagged external context covariates only. No R10 calibration, selection, or outcome-based update. Report all eligible, 30 common and 8 new country cohorts (eligible country counts may differ). This is known-data secondary transport, not untouched confirmation.

## Decomposition, uncertainty and contrary evidence
Compute exact weighted positive-negative AUC pair decomposition for frozen R10 predictions and new matched comparisons. Report diagonal pair mass, conditional diagonal and off-diagonal AUC and verify reconstruction to numerical precision. These are ranking-pair contributions, never variance shares.
Observed binary variance decomposes into between-country, between coarse socioeconomic cells within countries, and within-cell terms using the law of total variance. Cells use education (0–2,3–5,6–9), deprivation (<1,1–<2,>=2), and employment. These are analytical positions, not relational classes; sampling noise inflates apparent between-cell variation. Report discordance 2p(1-p), cell sizes and effective sample sizes. Null latent ICC is tau²/(tau²+pi²/3), not observed variance explained.
Country bootstrap (2,000 draws) for paired proper-score contrasts and descriptive variance; it conditions on fitted predictions. Country-cluster sandwich inference for FE logistic uses a small-cluster correction and t reference with J−1 degrees of freedom. Random-slope uncertainty and contexts rely on 34 contexts and are not made precise by respondent N. Report calibration intercept/slope descriptively, without using them to repair test predictions. Unweighted, complete common-four-outcome, occupation-adjusted, categorical education and item-threshold sensitivities will be run where data permit. Sensitivities and null results remain visible.

## H1–H6 decision rules
H1: distinct within/between associations and within-country proper-score improvement, allowing weak/null gradients. H2: pooled/within discrepancy accompanied by prevalence/offset information in matched baselines; pair decomposition alone is insufficient. H3: compare all four items also on common respondents; binary coding sensitivity limits substantive ranking. H4: education random-slope dispersion and education×institution association; a null interval or instability defeats strong moderation claims. H5: losses/calibration/prevalence across waves and common countries, not individual change. H6: nonzero observed disagreement within adequately populated analytical cells; this never isolates free will or stable individual residual variance.
